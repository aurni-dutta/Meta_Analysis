About these results: data and model assumptions

Data: FAO 2023 food-supply baselines and country-specific food-demand model workbooks.

Countries: Nigeria, Ghana, and South Africa.

Available scenarios: Own-price and Income. Cross-price and Tariff are still under development.

Scenario interpretation: The current own-price comparison applies a percentage change to each food group's own price separately. The national nutrient-impact calculation aggregates these modeled group-level changes.

Limitations: Results describe modeled national food supply, not measured individual dietary intake. Missing elasticity estimates are reported as unavailable rather than assumed to be zero.

Recommended nutrient intakes: Reference-value comparisons have not yet been implemented.

Each row represents 100% of that nutrient's estimated national food supply. These are food-supply contributions, not measured individual dietary intake or recommended intake targets.
